# Animated Data Heroine Prompt Pack

## 3D Poster Prompt

Take the facial reference from the uploaded image and create a hyper-detailed 3D stylized portrait poster of a confident female data superhero. The face must be fully rendered in 3D, not a real photo pasted onto a body. Preserve identity, including face shape, eyes, hair shape, and overall likeness, but re-render the person as a polished 3D character with stylized skin shading, sculpted hair, and cinematic rim lighting.

Subject: A powerful female data engineer superhero with a confident heroic expression, Pixar-meets-Unreal Engine 5 render style, semi-realistic, highly polished.

Pose: Standing on a futuristic skyscraper rooftop in “Git City.” One arm is raised high, with a glowing Python energy symbol floating above her open palm, surrounded by orange-red fire, sparks, and blue data particles.

Costume: Sleek modern superhero suit in dark navy, electric blue, and violet. Subtle circuit-board patterns glow across the chest and arms. A long flowing cape catches the wind.

Cape effect: Small glowing tech particles and mini logos break away from the cape edge: Python, AWS, Snowflake, Airflow, Spark, Docker, Power BI, and AI symbols, drifting upward like fireflies.

Background: Futuristic neon cyberpunk Git City at blue hour. Buildings resemble Git repository branches, commit graph towers, glowing branch lines, floating commit nodes, terminal-window billboards, and a subtle GitHub Octocat hologram in the skyline.

Lighting: Cinematic orange and electric-blue rim lighting, volumetric god rays, lens flare on the Python flame, vibrant saturated colors.

Format: Vertical portrait, 2:3 aspect ratio, hero centered slightly lower-third, clean negative space at top for a name banner.

Negative: no text, no watermark, no extra people, no distorted hands, no photographic face pasted onto 3D body.

## Veo Motion Prompt

Subtle cinematic animation, 5 seconds, seamless loop.

- Cape flows and ripples in slow-motion wind with realistic fabric physics
- Hair gently reacts to the wind
- Small glowing tech particles detach from the cape and float upward like embers
- Fire around the Python energy symbol flickers and swirls continuously
- Data particles travel through the skyline like moving pipeline streams
- Git City neon branch lights pulse softly
- Hero stays mostly still with subtle breathing
- Camera is static with no zoom or pan
- Smooth cinematic 24fps feel

Do not change the character face, pose, costume, or composition. Only animate cape, fire, particles, hair, neon glow, and background lights.
